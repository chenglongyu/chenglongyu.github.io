Syllabus

The course introduces complex manifolds, Kähler manifolds, Hodge structures, and variations of Hodge structures. Through examples of curves, K3 surfaces, Calabi–Yau manifolds, and families of hypersurfaces, we will study the corresponding period domains and period maps.
